Mama Music

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2 October 2026

1. Information We May Process

Mama Music may receive or process information made available through Discord when you interact with the Bot.

Depending on the feature being used, this may include your Discord user ID, Discord server/guild ID, Discord server name, Discord channel or voice-channel information required for music functionality, commands and interactions you send, music searches and URLs, music queue information, and technical information required for the Bot to operate.

Mama Music does not require you to create a separate Mama Music account.

2. Server Whitelist Information

Mama Music uses a server whitelist to control which Discord servers may use the Bot.

The Discord server/guild ID of an approved server may be stored for this purpose. The whitelist is used to determine whether Mama Music is permitted to operate within a particular Discord server.

3. Music Queue and Configuration Data

Mama Music may store information necessary to maintain music functionality, including music queue information and server-specific music state or configuration.

This may include Discord server ID, voice-channel ID, music track information, music URLs, queue information, and playback state necessary to maintain operation.

4. Music Searches

When you search for music through Mama Music, your search request may be sent to the relevant third-party music service used to provide search results.

For example, Mama Music may use third-party services such as YouTube or SoundCloud. Those services may independently process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Third-Party Services

Mama Music may interact with third-party services to provide functionality.

These may include Discord for Bot functionality and voice connections, YouTube where used for music searches or retrieval, and SoundCloud where used for music searches or retrieval.

Mama Music does not control the privacy practices of these third-party services.

6. How We Use Information

Information processed by Mama Music may be used to:

  • Provide music functionality.
  • Respond to Discord commands and interactions.
  • Manage server access and whitelisting.
  • Maintain music queues.
  • Maintain Bot configuration.
  • Troubleshoot errors.
  • Prevent abuse or unauthorized use.
  • Maintain security.
  • Improve reliability and functionality.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Data Retention

Mama Music retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is used, including maintaining functionality, security, configuration, and server whitelist records.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or otherwise removed from Mama Music's systems.

Some information may remain available through Discord or third-party services independently of Mama Music.

8. Data Sharing

Mama Music does not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services where necessary to provide requested functionality, such as performing a music search or accessing a requested music source.

Mama Music may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal requests, protect security, prevent abuse or fraud, or protect the rights or safety of users or others.

9. Discord

Mama Music operates through Discord.

Your use of Discord is governed separately by Discord's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. Mama Music does not control information collected directly by Discord.

10. Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information handled by Mama Music.

However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's Privacy

Mama Music is not specifically directed toward children.

Users must comply with Discord's age requirements and all applicable laws concerning use of Discord and third-party applications.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights concerning personal information, including rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

Requests concerning information controlled directly by Discord may need to be directed to Discord.

For information controlled by Mama Music, contact the Mama Music owner or support team through the Discord server where Mama Music is installed.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

The latest version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL associated with Mama Music.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to Mama Music, contact the Mama Music owner or support team through the Discord server where Mama Music is installed.
